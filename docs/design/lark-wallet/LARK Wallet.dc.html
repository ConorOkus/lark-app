LARK
Bitcoin that behaves like cash.
A self-custodial wallet on Ark, with Lightning underneath. Nothing about rounds, VTXOs, expiry, or exits reaches the surface — the wallet does that work and only speaks when a person must act.
DESIGN RULES
One balance. No spendable/pending/on-chain split. Money is money; the wallet picks the rail.
One gold moment. Gold marks the action about to happen — never twice on a screen.
Verdicts, not menus. "Sent." not "Your payment was processed."
Mechanics live in Advanced. Every real Ark number is one screen deep, never on the way to paying.
